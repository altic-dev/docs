FluidVoice is built around a local-first principle: your voice recordings, transcribed text, and AI activity never leave your Mac unless you explicitly choose a cloud provider. This page explains precisely what stays on your device, what optional analytics collect, and how cloud AI providers fit in when you choose to use them.

## What Stays on Your Mac

All of the following are processed and stored exclusively on your device:

- **Voice recordings and raw audio** — captured locally and never transmitted anywhere
- **Transcribed text** — the output of your speech recognition model, processed on-device
- **AI prompts and responses** — when using Fluid Intelligence, all prompt and response activity runs locally with no network calls
- **Custom dictionary entries and prompt profiles** — your personal vocabulary boosts and dictation prompt customizations
- **Transcription history** — your optional local recording history, stored on your Mac and exportable as a ZIP file

None of this data is sent to ALTIC or any third party.

## Optional Analytics

FluidVoice includes opt-in anonymous analytics to help understand app health and feature usage. Analytics are enabled by default, but you can disable them at any time.

### What analytics collect

Analytics are designed to be minimal and non-identifying:

- App version, build number, and macOS version
- Low-cardinality feature and configuration flags (for example: which app mode is active, which major settings are enabled)
- Approximate usage ranges — not exact values
- High-level success and error outcomes

### How to disable analytics

Go to **Settings → Share Anonymous Analytics** and toggle it off. Analytics stop immediately and no further data is sent.

## Cloud AI Providers

FluidVoice's core dictation is entirely local. However, if you choose to enhance your transcriptions using a cloud AI provider, your transcribed text is sent to that provider's API.

Supported providers:

- **OpenAI**
- **Groq**
- **Custom providers** — any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure

When you use a cloud provider, your transcription text leaves your Mac to reach that provider's servers. Review each provider's own privacy policy to understand how they handle your data.

**API keys** for cloud providers are stored securely in the macOS Keychain. When macOS prompts you for key access, select **Always Allow** to avoid repeated prompts.

### Fluid Intelligence stays local

If you use **Fluid Intelligence** (the Fluid-1 model), all AI enhancement—smart formatting, context-aware capitalization, adaptive tone, and post-processing—runs entirely on your Mac. No data leaves your device, no API key is needed, and no cloud provider is involved.
